Privacy Policy for Openclaw

Last updated: June 2026

Openclaw is a personal productivity tool used solely by its developer (David Rowan). It accesses Google account data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive) only to perform tasks explicitly requested by the account owner.

Data Collection

Openclaw does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. All data accessed via Google APIs is used in real time to complete requested tasks and is not retained.

Data Use

Google account data is accessed only at the direction of the account owner and is not shared with any third party.
